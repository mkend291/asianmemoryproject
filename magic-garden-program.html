Program Notes

Welcome
Magic Garden celebrates uplifting stories from two anime, and represents our growth as people, musicians and artists. Welcome to our garden — a space to cultivate our craft and share our stories, laughter and love for music.
Part I. Violet
Inspired by the anime series Violet Evergarden, we walk with the protagonist, Violet, on her journey to understand the meaning of the phrase “I love you.” Following a devastating war, Violet seeks to reintegrate into society by becoming an Automemory Doll. As a Doll, Violet travels the world to write on behalf of people with stories of grief, loss, compassion, and romance.

Our music accompanies Violet on her journey through her struggles and healing process. The flute begins on six notes to represent Violet’s musical motif. In the “Theme”, the piano mimics typewriter clicks and represents how Violet’s work as a Doll helped her grow into a confident and compassionate woman. In “The Ultimate Price”, Violet finds her grief overbearing as she fully realizes the disappearance of her mentor, Major Gilbert, in the final battle of the war. We end with “Across the Violet Sky” to celebrate the strength to tell someone how you truly feel.
Part II. Blue & Beyond
Imagine enchanting views, sailing the seas, and a charming flower field. In Part II, we join two mages and a warrior on a journey of a lifetime. Frieren defeats the Demon King with the Party of Heroes to establish an era of peace. Able to live thousands of years, the ancient elf outlives her companions and embarks on a new journey to understand human emotions. Frieren experiences flashbacks of her moments with Himmel, a warrior in her previous travel party, and yearns to reconnect with him. On a new journey with a warrior, Stark, and Fern, her protégé, Frieren encounters parallels to her time with the Party of Heroes and becomes a mentor and friend to her new companions.

How do we find meaning in time and relationships across lifetimes?

We formed Part II around “Waltz for Stark and Fern” as a nod to evolving symphonic structure. We depict the excitement of a new adventure with “Journey of a Lifetime” and travel through moments of triumph, dance, and sorrow. Inspired by the Dies Irae in Hector Berlioz’s Symphonie Fantastique, “Fractured”, an original by Daniel Kim, begins with a somber, modal duet between the flute and violin, then spirals into despair and climaxes in an epic battle between a mage versus a monster. Of course, we emerge victorious, as there are no monsters too strong for an ancient mage.
From the Arranger:
Magic Garden is a reimagining of the two anime through a fresh musical journey grounded in familiar musical structures. To achieve this, we approached the original soundtrack as a source of inspiration. Rather than transcribing note for note, Evan Call’s musical themes are interwoven throughout the arrangements or they were used to create new, original music altogether. Harmonizations or canonic gestures in the flute and violin create musical conversations, imitating the dialogue between characters. Classical and Romantic Era musical forms, such as symphonic or sonata form, influence our musical structure and support the emotional arc of our journey. Characters are introduced in the first movements of Part I and Part II. Middle movements consist of the waltz, adagios, and scherzos to portray character development, turmoil, and landmark moments of these stories. Our final “Medley” reiterates the main theme and aligns with the triumphant nature and compound time signature found in many final symphonic movements.
